# Supplychainer

Plan the fastest, best-value and most reliable way to ship between 444 ports, airports and
logistics hubs. Try a sample trip, simulate a disruption such as the Suez Canal blockage, and
see the route change around it.

- Delay predictions come from quantile machine-learning models (typical, planned and worst-case arrival).
- News headlines are scored for severity and relevance to each transport mode.
- Watch a route and turn on a disruption to see a live alert with a suggested new plan.

This is a public demo: everyone shares the same saved routes and alerts, the database resets when
the Space restarts, and webhooks are turned off.
